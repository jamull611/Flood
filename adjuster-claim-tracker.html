Blocked dates (no inspections)

AdStrat sync

✓ Chrome extension connected. Click ⟳ Sync from AdStrat on the Claims tab any time you're signed in to AdStrat in Chrome.

Works while Chrome is open on this computer and you're signed in to AdStrat (tick Remember Me). If the tracker isn't open, it opens briefly in a background tab, syncs, and closes. With cloud sync on, the claims then show up on your phone. If AdStrat signs you out, the extension's icon shows a !; click it to sign back in.

On your phone, a bookmark copies your claims list from AdStrat, then ⟳ Sync from AdStrat on the Claims tab brings them in. Set the bookmark up once:

It's short and always runs the latest version. If tapping the bookmark says it couldn't load, open the full-code page in Chrome and use that code instead. It also has a tiny test bookmark if nothing happens at all.

iPhone (Safari)
  1. Tap Copy bookmark code above.
  2. In Safari, open any page (AdStrat is fine), tap Share (square with arrow) → Add Bookmark. Name it Copy AdStrat claims and tap Save.
  3. Tap the Bookmarks icon (open book) → Edit → tap Copy AdStrat claims. Tap its address, delete it, and Paste the code. Tap Done.

Each time: in Safari, sign in to AdStrat and open your claims list. Tap Bookmarks → Copy AdStrat claims → Copy for tracker. Then open this app → Claims → ⟳ Sync from AdStrat and tap Paste if asked.

Android (Chrome)
  1. Tap Copy bookmark code above.
  2. In Chrome, open any page (AdStrat is fine), tap ⋮ → ☆ to bookmark it.
  3. Tap ⋮ → Bookmarks, then ⋮ next to the new bookmark → Edit. Name it Copy AdStrat claims, replace the URL by pasting the code, and go back to save.

Each time: in Chrome, sign in to AdStrat and open your claims list. Tap the address bar, type Copy AdStrat and tap the bookmark (★) in the suggestions → Copy for tracker. Then open this app → Claims → ⟳ Sync from AdStrat and allow pasting if asked.

Install the Chrome extension once, and the Sync button pulls straight from AdStrat whenever you're signed in there. Your AdStrat password is never stored, and nothing in AdStrat is ever changed.

  1. In Chrome, go to chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode (top right).
  2. Click Load unpacked and choose this folder:
    claim-tracker-adstrat-extension (next to this tracker file)
  3. On the extension's card, click Details and turn on Allow access to file URLs.
  4. Refresh this page. This panel will say connected.
No extension? Use the bookmark instead
  1. Drag this button onto your Chrome bookmarks bar (Ctrl+Shift+B shows the bar):
    📋 Copy AdStrat claims
  2. On your AdStrat claims list, click the bookmark, then Copy for tracker.
  3. Back here, click ⟳ Sync from AdStrat.

New claims are added. Claims already in the tracker are matched by AdStrat ID, or by claim # and insured name the first time, and updated: reserves, inspection date, status (forward only) and AdStrat stage. Your notes, contact log and Paid/Closed statuses are never changed.
